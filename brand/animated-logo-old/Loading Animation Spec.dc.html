Proteus — Loading Animation

Frame sequence spec, derived from the base logo mark.

Concept

The hatched triangle of the mark is made of 19 parallel diagonal bands. In the loading animation, exactly one band at a time fades to 60% opacity while the rest stay solid, sweeping continuously from one corner to the other — the surface reads as if it's still being rendered.

Frame parameters

  • Frame count: 19 (one per band, looping).
  • Canvas: 104 × 144 units (13:18 portrait), exported at 208 × 288px (2x).
  • Band width: 8 units; band pitch (spacing): 10.5 × √2 ≈ 14.85 units, laid on the 45° diagonal.
  • Active-frame opacity: the single active band drops to 0.6 opacity; all other bands stay at 1.0.
  • Suggested playback: 90ms per frame (≈11fps), looping forward continuously. At 19 frames that's a ~1.7s loop.
