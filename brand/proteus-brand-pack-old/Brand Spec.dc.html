Proteus — Logo Spec

Construction, color, and typography reference for the Proteus mark.

Concept

The mark is a portrait quad split into its two constituent triangles — the base primitive of a GPU render pipeline. One triangle renders solid; the other carries a diagonal hatch, reading as a textured / in-progress surface. The pairing nods to Proteus's core idea: components that are rendered, and metamorphic — never static.

Geometry

Drawn on a 104 × 144 unit grid (aspect ratio 13:18, portrait). Two triangles split by the diagonal from the top-left corner (0,0) to the bottom-right corner (104,144):

  • Triangle A (solid) — points (0,0), (0,144), (104,144): the bottom-left half.
  • Triangle B (hatched) — points (0,0), (104,0), (104,144): the top-right half.
  • A 2px seam line runs along the shared diagonal, colored to match the surrounding background (so it reads as a gap between the two faces, not a stroke).

Minimum clear space around the mark: half the mark's width on all sides. Minimum size: 20px tall in digital use (favicon/app-icon exceptions aside).

The hatch fill

Built as an SVG <pattern>, tiled 10.5 × 10.5 units, rotated 45°. Each tile is a solid square in the triangle's own color with one vertical line stroked in the background color, 5 units wide — so the "gaps" between hatch lines show through as background, and the lines are widely spaced (roughly 2× the line's own width).

<pattern id="hatch" patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse"
         width="10.5" height="10.5" patternTransform="rotate(45)">
  <rect width="10.5" height="10.5" fill="{triangleColor}"/>
  <line x1="0" y1="0" x2="0" y2="10.5"
        stroke="{backgroundColor}" stroke-width="5"/>
</pattern>
<polygon points="0,0 104,0 104,144" fill="url(#hatch)"/>

On a light surface, {triangleColor} is the mark's ink and {backgroundColor} is the page background. On a dark surface, swap only which color plays which role — the fill is always the triangle's assigned color, the lines are always the surface's background color.

Typography

Wordmark: Inter, weight 700, all caps, letter-spacing 0.06em, set in the same color as the mark. Minimum companion mark height: keep the mark's cap-height roughly equal to the wordmark's cap-height, gap of 12–16px between mark and wordmark at logo-lockup scale.

Variants & usage

  • Color / light — Deep Violet mark on Off-white or any light surface.
  • Color / dark — Light Violet mark on Near-black or any dark surface.
  • Monochrome / on white — Ink mark, for single-color print or constrained UI contexts.
  • Monochrome / on black — Off-white mark on Pure black, for dark single-color contexts.

Don't recolor the mark outside this set, don't separate the two triangles, don't add drop shadows or outlines, don't stretch off the 13:18 aspect ratio.
